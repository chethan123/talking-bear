# Device notes

Results of on-device experiments (the X-… experiments of plan §5 and the owner tasks), per phone and Chrome version. For each run, copy the template below, fill in every field (raw numbers under Observations), and append it under **Results** as `### <Txx> — <YYYY-MM-DD>`, newest last. A result that contradicts the plan also needs an ADR in `docs/adr/` and a plan update.

Never paste or attach captured or real-voice audio here (plan Q2): numbers and links to feature traces only.

## Template

```text
Date:
Task/experiment id:
Phone (model, Android version):
Chrome version (chrome://version):
Setup:
Steps:
Observations (raw numbers):
Conclusion:
Plan impact (none / ADR link):
```
