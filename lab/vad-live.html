What this page is

Plan §7 M2: the live end-to-end check on D1 that covers what feature traces can't, such as the LSTM reset at every re-arm and the pre-gate warm-up. The bear above is the app itself, booted by src/main.ts with the debug overlay: the same engine, worker, VAD, voice chain and session, nothing copied. Only aggregate counts are kept.

How to run the check

Tap the bear to wake it and allow the microphone. Choose the condition (quiet, fan or TV noise), then say short phrases (under 3 s, with at least 2 s between them) and wait for each replay. After each phrase press exactly one of Hit (the bear replayed the whole phrase, once), Miss (no replay) or Split (the phrase came back in two or more replays). Press Truncated as well when a replay cut off the start or the end of the phrase, and False trigger whenever the bear recorded with nobody speaking. Undo takes back the last mark. Phrases spoken is hits + misses + splits.

The auto counters come from the session's transitions: recordings (Listening → Recording), completed (Processing → Playing), misfires (a segment too short to keep: Recording → Listening) and maxLength ends (a segment cut at the 10 s cap: Recording → Processing). Each take's counts go to the condition selected when it started; marks go to the condition selected when pressed. Check the two rare counters once on the device: in quiet, knock on the table (if the VAD starts on the knock at all, it ends as a misfire, with no replay), then speak for more than 10 s without a pause (a maxLength end, then the replay). The M2 exit criterion: at most 1 miss or split in 30 phrases in each of quiet, fan and TV, with the frozen parameters. At the end press Copy summary and paste the JSON into docs/device-notes.md, with the phone model and the Android and Chrome versions.

The debug overlay turns the worker's feature trace on while it is shown, and while the trace is on the Silero pre-gate never skips a frame (Silero runs on every frame). The line at the top of the tally says whether that applies. To measure the pre-gate's skipping and warm-up once it is enabled, open this page as vad-live.html?debug=0, which boots the app without the overlay.

Privacy

Plan Q2: only aggregate counts are kept, as integers in this page's memory. The page keeps no audio, no times and no record of single phrases, stores nothing in the browser and sends nothing anywhere: Copy summary puts the counts on the clipboard, and leaving or reloading the page resets them. The app itself never stores audio: each take lives in memory only until its replay ends.
