Silent-sink suspension vs capture gaps

X-R4 (plan §5, §3.2): does connecting the capture worklet's zero output to destination (graph B) let Chrome release the output device while the app only listens, without losing captured audio, and what does resuming the output cost? Pick the graph (changing it reloads the page with the choice in the URL), press Start, and play a steady tone of about 911 Hz (not a multiple of 100 Hz: at 48 kHz a 1 kHz tone repeats every 48 frames, so a dropped 4, 5 or 10 ms capture buffer leaves no step) from a second device near the phone. Keep the tone at least 30 dB above the room noise, and keep the screen on for the whole run: a hidden page's timers run about once a minute after 5 minutes, so a take would outgrow the 12 s capture ring and fail.

Every second the page takes the newest audio from the capture worklet's ring and looks for discontinuities (a step between neighbouring samples larger than 3× the steepest step of the block's tone, A·2πf/sr, with A = RMS·√2 and f from zero crossings) and for runs of at least 1 ms of exact zeros. Consecutive takes neither overlap nor leave a gap, so every captured frame is analysed exactly once, from 1 s after the first captured sample that isn't an exact zero (the mic delivers a few tens of ms of zeros as it starts, and its first buffers can jump). Run graph A first as the control, with the same tone, level, load and room. Graph A has no suspension, so a discontinuity there comes from the setup (tone too quiet, room noise, handling); graph B's result is then inconclusive, not a FAIL. Then run graph B for the same time: keep the phone silent for at least 40 s first, so the silent-sink suspender has released the output (adb shell dumpsys media.audio_flinger shows no active output track then). Turn the load generator on for both runs.

Gate (plan §5): enable graph B only if there are 0 gaps longer than 20 ms in 10 minutes with the sink suspended. A steady tone can't show how much audio a discontinuity lost, and the ring holds 80 Hz high-passed audio, so a short input dropout leaves steps rather than exact zeros: every discontinuity counts as a gap of unknown length, as do a zero run longer than 20 ms and a take that failed (frames that couldn't be analysed). The verdict is FAIL as soon as one shows up, and PASS only after 10 min 40 s of analysed audio without one (the first 40 s let the sink suspend).

Resume latency: after at least 40 s without output (Since last output), Play click after idle plays a 1 ms click through the app's master bus and measures the wall time until getOutputTimestamp().contextTime passes the click's start time, then does the same for a second click at once; the value in brackets is the same moment read from the timestamp itself, without the polling delay. The mic hears the clicks, so the gate is frozen at the first click test: run it after the gap measurement.

Privacy (plan Q2): the clip the page receives is zero-filled at once; a take still being assembled when it is superseded (after a timeout) or when the mic closes is dropped, and the engine's chunk copies of a take over 48 000 frames are dropped unzeroed (memory only; nothing is stored). Only counts are kept. Nothing captured is stored or exported, the graph choice lives in the URL only, and Copy log copies text only.
