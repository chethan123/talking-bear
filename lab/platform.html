Backgrounding, sample rates and wake lock

Every line of the log carries the local wall-clock time and performance.now() in ms. The page logs visibilitychange (with visibilityState), pagehide, pageshow, freeze and resume from the moment it loads.

X-E6 (plan §5): press Start capture (echo cancellation off, the app's constraints). The input RMS is logged once a second, with ctx.state and the track state, and so are the track's mute, unmute and ended events and every context state change. Go Home for 10 s and for 6 min, and lock and unlock the screen; note which mic notification Android shows. The capture keeps running until Stop: nothing stops it when the page is hidden.

X-E10: press Probe rates. For each constraint set in turn (all three off; EC on; noiseSuppression only) it opens a new context and the mic, waits 1 s, logs ctx.sampleRate, baseLatency, outputLatency and track.getSettings() on one line, and closes both. ctx.sampleRate is the context (output) rate, the same for every set because the context is created before getUserMedia, as in the app; each set's capture rate is the sampleRate inside track.getSettings().

X-E11: press Acquire wake lock, lock and unlock the screen, and toggle battery saver. The lock follows the app's policy (src/platform/wake-lock.ts): it is re-requested whenever the page becomes visible while wanted. Every request, grant, release event and rejection is logged, with its message.

Privacy (plan Q2): no audio is kept. The capture logs only RMS numbers, the log stays in memory, and Copy log copies text only.
