Does Fenn's voice sound like a duck, and can you still understand it?

X-F4 (duck plan §5 T117, §6): first the owner pre-tunes the Duckling voice alone (pitch ratio, AM rate, AM mix and the 1.6 kHz peak, within the bounds the tests cover) and copies the values. Then the listening test: the page shows the speaker a word and its picture; the speaker holds Record and says it; the listener, who can't see the screen, hears it in one of three voices (Bear cub, the frozen duck, or a softer duck) and says what they heard. The experimenter taps ✓ or ✗, then answers "sounds like a duck?". Listeners get codes L1, L2, … from the page, never names.

Privacy (plan Q2): only the answers are kept. Each recording lives in memory for one trial (the pre-tune recording until the next one, Forget recording or Close) and is zeroed afterwards, on Close and when you leave the page. Nothing captured is stored or exported; Copy params, Copy results and Copy log copy text only.
