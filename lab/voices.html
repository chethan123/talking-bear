Which voice effect do you like best?

X-F1 (plan §5): a blind listening test of three ways to raise the pitch of your own voice: linear playbackRate, the app's sinc resampler, and formant-preserving PSOLA, at a pitch ratio of 1.4, 1.7 or 2.0 picked at random for each trial. Enter the listener's label (for example "adult 1" or "child 1"), choose the output route (phone speaker or headphones), and press Open mic (the app's own constraints). Then, for each trial: hold Record, say a short phrase (at most 4 s), and release. The page plays three versions as X1, X2 and X3, in a random order, 600 ms apart, all loudness-matched. Tap your favourite, or All the same if you can't choose; Replay plays them again. Do several trials per listener and route, then open the results below and press Copy results (JSON).

Privacy (plan Q2): only votes are kept. Your phrase and its three versions live in memory for one trial and are zeroed after the vote, on a new recording, on Close and when you leave the page. Nothing captured is stored or exported; Copy results and Copy log copy text only.
