VAD trace: real-voice feature traces

Plan §7 M2: records real-voice feature traces on D1, the primary source for tuning the VAD. The page listens only: the app's own microphone constraints (all browser processing off), Silero on every 32 ms frame, and nothing is ever played. Every 10 ms it keeps the raw and the band-limited level and the voicing score, every 32 ms the Silero speech probability, and the hold-to-talk marks on the same frame clock.

Choose the session (A for tuning, B for gating), the condition, the placement, the speaker and the script. Each cell's script is preselected: S1 to S6 hold 30 short phrases each, and noise is the 15 min noise-only recording (switch to it by hand for fan and TV noise). Press Start and keep this page in front: a wake lock keeps the screen on, and if the wake lock is refused (battery saver), set the screen timeout to 30 min first. Hiding the page, a muted or lost microphone, a stopped audio context or a stalled trace ends the recording with an error, and that trace can't be exported. Hold the big button, or a key of a Bluetooth page-turner (Space, Page Down, Page Up, Right arrow or Enter), for the whole of each phrase: press just before you start speaking and let go just after you finish. The next phrase appears 2 s after you let go. Press Stop at the end: the page records 1.5 s more, then stops. The noise-only recording stops by itself after 15 min.

Export saves 2 files; if Chrome asks to download multiple files, choose Allow, then check that both trace-….json and trace-….bin are in Downloads. They are named trace-<session>-<condition>-<placement>-<speaker>-<UTC time>; rename them to trace.json and trace.bin in their own folder test/traces/session-<A|B>/<condition>-<placement>[-child]/ (scripts/vad-eval.ts finds only trace.json). Export stays available until Discard or the next Start, and the setup is locked until then. A run stopped before the end of its script still exports, but the line next to Export flags it as incomplete: not a protocol trace.

Privacy (plan Q2): feature traces only, never audio. The page never cuts a clip from the capture ring, and levels, voicing scores and speech probabilities can't be turned back into speech. Nothing is stored in the browser: the two trace files leave the page only when Export is pressed, and leaving the page discards everything.
