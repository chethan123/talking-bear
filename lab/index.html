Talking Bear lab pages

Dev-only experiment pages (plan §5), served by pnpm dev and never built into dist. How to run them on the phone: docs/lab.md. No lab page stores or exports captured audio (plan Q2).

  • Routing: X-E1 output routing with the mic open, X-E2 input spectrum
  • Sound effects: every reaction sound, with its duration and peak
  • Bench: X-V2 Silero TS vs ORT per-frame timing, worklet DSP per quantum
  • Platform: X-E6 backgrounding and screen lock, X-E10 sample rates and latencies, X-E11 wake lock
  • Rig: channel sliders, the SVG DOM debug view beside the canvas, X-A1 stress
  • Spectrum: X-F2 energy above 8 kHz of spoken phrases, statistics only
  • Latency: X-E5 loopback click latency vs the output-latency prediction, echo tail → cooldown
  • Continuity: X-R4 silent-sink suspension vs capture gaps (graph A/B, under load), output resume latency
  • Voices: X-F1 blind A/B/X of linear, sinc and PSOLA pitch shifting at 1.4, 1.7 and 2.0, votes only
  • Gestures: X-A7 labelled pointer traces (tap, long-press, pet, tickle) for the recogniser's confusion matrix
  • Envelope: X-A3 jaw, width, round and teeth of spoken phrases with the band edges × p and fixed, statistics only
  • VAD trace: M2 real-voice feature traces on D1 (listen-only, Silero on every frame, hold-to-talk labels); exports trace.json and trace.bin, never audio
  • VAD live: M2 live end-to-end check on D1, the production app with the debug overlay and a tally panel; copies aggregate counts only, never audio
