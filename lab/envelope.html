Mouth envelope of spoken phrases

X-A3 (plan §5, §4.6.2): after the app's voice chain, do vowels separate in the mouth's width and round tracks, and should the analysis band edges scale with the pitch ratio p? Pick a voice, press Open mic (the app's own constraints: all browser processing off), keep or type the phrase, then press the button, wait about half a second, say the phrase, wait about half a second and release (at most 5 s). The page runs the app's voice chain on the phrase and plots its jaw, width, round and teeth tracks twice: with the band edges × p, as the app does, and with the fixed edges. The table keeps the mean jaw, width and round over the phrase's vowel-dominant frames (voiced, and within 15 dB of its loudest frame). Say the 10 suggested phrases with each voice, then press Copy table.

Privacy (plan Q2): each phrase is held in memory only while the button is down and while it is processed, then zeroed at once. A hold that is interrupted, or a page that is hidden, discards the phrase unprocessed. Only the latest phrase's envelope (for the plot) and the per-phrase means are kept. Nothing captured is stored or exported; Copy table and Copy log copy text only.
