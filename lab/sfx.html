Sound effects

One button per reaction sound (plan §4.6.4). Each plays through the master bus (gain 0.7) and the sfx scheduler, as in the app. Beside each: its duration and its sample peak, from an offline render at this context's sample rate (the cap is −9 dBFS). Tune a recipe's constants in src/audio/sfx-synths.ts; the page reloads.
