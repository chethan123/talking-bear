Energy above 8 kHz in real captures

X-F2 (plan §5): how much energy a real capture carries above 8 kHz, which shows how audible aliasing would have been without the sinc resampler. Press Open mic (the app's own constraints: all browser processing off). Then, for each phrase: press, wait about half a second, say the phrase, wait about half a second, release. The page shows the energy above 8 kHz and above 12 kHz relative to the total, over the louder half of the 10 ms frames, and their median, p90 and max over the session. Do all 20 phrases on each phone, then press Copy results (JSON). The device label can carry a name you gave a Bluetooth headset: check it before you paste the results anywhere.

Privacy (plan Q2): each phrase is held in memory only while the button is down; it is analysed on release and then zeroed at once. A hold that is interrupted, or a page that is hidden, discards the phrase unanalysed. Only the numbers are kept. Nothing captured is stored or exported; Copy results and Copy log copy text only.
